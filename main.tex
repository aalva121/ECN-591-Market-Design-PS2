\section*{Interpretation}

Looking at Table 5, Deferred Acceptance (DA) has the lowest ranked average of 1.213 out of the three matching mechanisms with Immediate Acceptance (IA) ranked as the second lowest with 1.270 and Top Trading Cycles (TTC) ranked as the highest average with 4.000. An average DA of 1.213 shows that students do not receive optimal assignments under this mechanism compared to IA which is slightly better and TTC which has a more optimal outcome.
